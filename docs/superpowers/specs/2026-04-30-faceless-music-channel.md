## Strategist's pushback (captured 2026-04-30)

**Two assumptions baked in:**
1. **YouTube monetization will accept this content.** Risky: YouTube has been tightening on AI-generated content + faceless meme accounts. CPM on instrumental/meme music is typically ~$0.50–2.00 — requires massive view volume to matter financially. Channel could be demonetized within 60 days due to "low quality" or "AI content" policy flags.
2. **Faceless channels are compatible with the DirtySnatcha brand.** Risky: running a faceless channel UNDER the artist brand could dilute the artist identity. A separate channel + brand persona is probably required, which doubles the operational surface.

**Failure mode:** spending 40 hours building the production pipeline before discovering YouTube demonetizes the channel. Meme music channels die fast — the algo cycles them.

**PURSUE/KILL QUESTION (open):**
> Is this:
> (a) **monetize the existing DSR catalog** via faceless channel format (low risk, fast ship, leverages assets you already own — could be done in weeks), OR
> (b) **build a NEW gen-AI music product / SaaS** (high risk, big swing, frontier-lab competition — 6-month bet)?

These are completely different projects with different scope, capital requirements, and risk profiles.

**Examples of crisp answers that would unblock this:**
- "(a) — leverage existing DirtySnatcha catalog as inventory, separate faceless channel with its own brand persona, post 3x/day for 60 days, kill if CPM stays under $1." → real spec.
- "(b) — generate AI music using a fine-tuned Stable Audio model, build the SaaS product where users buy AI-generated tracks for their own faceless channels, separate from DSR." → bigger spec with much more market validation needed.

**Example NON-crisp answers (would NOT unblock):**
- "Both" — without a clear primary, this is two projects pretending to be one.
- "Whatever makes the most money" — research question, not project scope.
- "I want to do faceless music" — vibe, not a project.
