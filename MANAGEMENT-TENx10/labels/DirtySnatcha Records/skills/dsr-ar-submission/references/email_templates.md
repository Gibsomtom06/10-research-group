# A&R Email Templates

## Acceptance Email

Subject: Welcome to DirtySnatcha Records — [Artist Name]

```
[Artist Name],

Your submission of "[Track Title]" has been reviewed and approved by the DirtySnatcha Records team.

We're excited to work with you. Here's what happens next:

1. You'll receive a contract for review within 48 hours
2. Once signed, you'll get access to the Project Room — this is where we collaborate on WAVs, stems, and marketing plans
3. We'll build a release timeline together: Single → EP → LP

Your portal access has been upgraded to Signed Artist. Log in at dirtysnatcharecords.com to see your dashboard.

Welcome to the family.

— DirtySnatcha Records
```

## Rejection Email

Subject: DirtySnatcha Records — Submission Update

```
[Artist Name],

Thanks for sending "[Track Title]" our way. We gave it a proper listen.

After review, we've decided not to move forward with this one right now. [1-2 sentences of honest, specific feedback — what worked, what didn't, what could improve].

Opinions are like assholes — everyone has one. This is ours today, and it doesn't mean yours won't change our mind tomorrow.

You're welcome to resubmit after 90 days. Your portal account stays active so you can track future submissions.

Keep creating.

— DirtySnatcha Records
```

## Waitlist Email

Subject: DirtySnatcha Records — Your Track is on the Waitlist

```
[Artist Name],

We reviewed "[Track Title]" and we like what we heard. We're not ready to commit to a full release right now, but we've added your track to our Compilation Queue.

What this means: when we're building our next compilation release, your track is in the running. We'll reach out if and when a slot opens up.

Your portal status has been updated to Waitlisted. You can check in anytime at dirtysnatcharecords.com.

Stay tuned.

— DirtySnatcha Records
```
