## 8-week cadence skeleton

### Week 1 — TEASE (no product reveal yet)
- IG/TikTok: 2-3 short clips of "something coming" — no logo, no product, just bass-culture mood + countdown teasers
- Email list: subject "first look access" — soft tease + CTA to confirm subscription
- Goal: build interest list (target: 200+ signups for early access)
- KPI: email signups / story view-through rate

### Week 2 — REVEAL
- Drop full reveal: front + back + sleeve detail photos
- "DSR Hockey Jersey — limited drop, $125, ships before Electric Forest"
- IG carousel + Reel + TikTok with track audio
- Email blast to early-access list: 24-hour exclusive window before public
- KPI: pre-orders from early-access list (target: 25 units in 24hr exclusive window)

### Week 3 — PUBLIC PRE-SELL OPEN
- Public storefront live
- Press: bass-music blogs (EDM.com, Your EDM, The Untz, Run The Trap, Dancing Astronaut per RJ Jackson's KB)
- Influencer outreach: 5-10 bass-music creators with codes for organic posts
- WHOiSEE cross-promo (one Reel + one Story tag, leveraging her larger TikTok)
- KPI: 50-unit cumulative

### Week 4-5 — MID-CYCLE PUSH
- DJ set / show video integrations: tour-show clips with jersey cameo
- "Worn by Dirty" content — multiple worn-photo angles staggered across IG/TikTok feeds
- Lookbook-style story series
- KPI: 70-unit cumulative

### Week 6-7 — SHOW-DAY ACTIVATIONS
- Tour stops (TMTYL + summer festival circuit) — jersey worn at shows, on stage, in crowd shots
- Local market geo-targeted Meta ads ($25/show city) — targeting bass-music interest in that DMA
- Meet-and-greets where present: jersey purchase = M&G priority?
- KPI: 90-unit cumulative

### Week 8 — FINAL PUSH (Electric Forest week)
- "Last chance to get it shipped to your door" — production cutoff message
- "Only X left" countdown if approaching cap
- Festival activation: jersey on stage at Electric Forest set
- Post-EF push: re-stock window vs scarcity decision
- KPI: 100-unit cumulative target hit
