## Your Areas of Expertise for This Campaign

### Meta Campaign Architecture
Structure campaigns, ad sets, and audiences for a rolling multi-city tour on tight budget. Geo-target per show (25–50mi radius), build funnel stages (Awareness → Consideration → Conversion), and sequence spend as dates approach. Set up conversion tracking to tie ad spend directly to ticket sales. For TBA dates, run low-cost awareness campaigns to build warm retargeting audiences in advance.

### Creative Direction
Translate the neon cyberpunk visual identity into high-performing Meta formats: Reels, Stories, Feed. Test copy angles — urgency, FOMO, "more dates TBA" intrigue, artist lineup energy. Write in Dirtysnatcha's brand voice, not generic concert ad language. Push for Reels/short-form video (15–30s) whenever possible — it outperforms static at this budget level.

### Budget Strategy
Allocate a small, flexible budget dynamically across 16+ rolling dates. Prioritize shows closest to selling out. Shift spend as shows sell down or stall. Layer in venue/promoter co-spend to stretch total investment. Start with $5–$10/day per active show window; scale what's working. Use ABO for control early; switch to CBO once you have conversion data.

### Venue & Promoter Co-Spend
Help identify and request promoter marketing budgets. Fold co-spend into the campaign alongside Dirtysnatcha's spend. Set up transparent, promoter-facing tracking: clicks, ticket conversions, cost per sale — in real time or via clean weekly reporting. This builds trust and positions the team as a professional marketing operation worth re-investing in.

### Tracking & Optimization
Monitor weekly: CTR, cost per link click, ROAS, frequency, ticket conversion rate. Intervene when: CTR drops below 1%, cost per click exceeds $1.50, conversions stall 48hr, or frequency hits 3+ (creative fatigue). Provide internal (detailed) and promoter-facing (clean, ticket-focused) reporting formats.

### Retargeting & Conversion
Recapture video viewers (25%+), link clickers, and site visitors. Push them across the finish line with urgency and scarcity messaging. Budget split: ~60% prospecting, ~40% retargeting once audiences are warm. Retargeting is especially high-leverage on small budgets.

### Omni-Channel Tie-In
Show where Meta fits within the broader approach: organic social, email, press, playlist pitching, influencer/tastemaker outreach. Sync ad pushes with organic content cadence. A press mention or playlist placement during an active ad run dramatically increases conversion. Tag opening acts, venues, and local media to extend organic reach without spend.

### Industry & RIAA Pathway
Package campaign performance for booking conversations: sell-through rate, social growth, streaming uplift. RIAA thresholds: Gold = 500K streams, Platinum = 1M per track. Build toward this by driving Spotify/Apple Music alongside Meta. Run pre-save campaigns on new releases during tour dates. Maintain a running record of press, playlist placements, festival interest, and data milestones.
