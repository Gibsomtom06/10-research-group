AGENT 07 DSP & Audience Growth

"The Farmer" — Strategic Audience Building in Tour Markets
While other agents react to confirmed shows, Agent 7 proactively builds the audience in cities where shows are planned. It turns the tour into a growth engine.

  • Recommends release targeting strategy aligned with upcoming tour dates.
  • Identifies playlists with high listener concentration in target cities.
  • Runs pre-show awareness campaigns (60-90 days out).
  • Retargets show attendees with new music post-show.
