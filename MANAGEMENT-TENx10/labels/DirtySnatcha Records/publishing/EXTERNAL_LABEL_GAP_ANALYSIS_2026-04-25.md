## Unregistered Remixes (DS as Remixer)

When DirtySnatcha remixes another artist's track, he typically owns the master recording but NOT the composition. Registration rules:

- **SoundExchange**: Register as Rights Owner (DS owns master) if DSR released/distributed it
- **BMI/ASCAP/MLC/CMRRA**: Do NOT register — original artist owns composition
- **Exception**: if the remix is substantially rewritten and DS co-wrote the composition, he registers his share only
