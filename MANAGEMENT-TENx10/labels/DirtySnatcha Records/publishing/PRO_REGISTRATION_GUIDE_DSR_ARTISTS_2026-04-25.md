# PRO Registration Guide — DSR Artists Without a PRO

**Date:** 2026-04-25  
**Context:** DSR contracts assign 100% of the Publisher's Share to DSR Publishing (ASCAP or BMI). Artists retain 100% of the Writer's Share — but only collect it if they are registered with a PRO. Artists without a PRO lose their writer's share entirely. This guide covers how to get registered fast.

---

## Why This Matters

When a DSR track streams or gets performed:

- **Publisher's Share (~50% of performance royalties)** → goes to DSR Publishing (already handled)
- **Writer's Share (~50% of performance royalties)** → goes to each songwriter, collected via their PRO

If a writer has no PRO account, their writer's share is either held or forfeited. It does NOT go to DSR — it simply sits uncollected or is distributed to other registered writers.

**This must be done BEFORE or immediately AFTER release, not months later. PROs do not pay retroactively in most cases.**

---

## Which PRO to Join

| Situation | Recommended PRO | Cost | Time to Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| US-based artist | BMI | Free | Same day (online) |
| US-based artist (alternative) | ASCAP | $50 one-time | 1–3 business days |
| Canadian artist | SOCAN | Free | Same day (online) |
| UK artist | PRS for Music | £10/year | 1–3 days |
| Australian artist | APRA AMCOS | Free | Same day |

**BMI is recommended for most DSR artists** — free, faster setup, and DSR's own BMI publisher (LAB10) makes cross-registration easier.

---

## Step-by-Step: BMI Registration

**Time required: 10–15 minutes online**

1. Go to **bmi.com/creators**
2. Click "Become a BMI Writer"
3. Fill out: Legal name, stage name, address, SSN (US artists) or ITIN
4. Submit — you'll receive a **CAE/IPI number** within 1–5 business days
5. Once you have your IPI, log into the BMI portal and register each composition:
   - Title, writer splits (your % + co-writers), publisher name (DSR Publishing → "LAB10 Publishing, BMI, IPI 1262829440")
   - ISRC if available
6. BMI will contact DSR Publishing (LAB10) to confirm the publisher share

### What to tell DSR:
Once your IPI number arrives, send it to thomas@dirtysnatcha.com so DSR can:
- Register your writer split on every track you wrote
- Confirm you in the BMI system as co-writer where applicable

---

## Step-by-Step: ASCAP Registration

**Use if you prefer ASCAP (matches DSR Publishing ASCAP account)**

1. Go to **ascap.com/music-creators/join**
2. Click "Join as a Writer"
3. Pay the $50 one-time fee
4. Fill out: Legal name, stage name, contact info, SSN
5. Receive IPI number (usually within 3–5 business days)
6. Register compositions at ascap.com with writer splits and ISRC
7. Publisher: "DirtySnatcha Records Publishing (ASCAP), IPI 1238282844"

## After Registration: Composition Registration Checklist

Once an artist has a PRO account and IPI number:

- [ ] DSR registers the composition(s) at BMI or ASCAP with correct writer splits
- [ ] ISRC added to composition registration
- [ ] ISWC requested (auto-assigned by BMI/ASCAP, takes ~30 days)
- [ ] Track added to DSR publishing spreadsheet with IPI confirmed
- [ ] If track has co-writers on OTHER PROs (e.g. SOCAN, PRS): make sure each PRO's system reflects the affiliation — cross-PRO matching happens automatically but can take 60–90 days

## What DSR DOES NOT Do

- DSR does NOT collect writer's share for artists — only publisher's share
- DSR does NOT register artists at their PRO — artists must do this themselves
- DSR DOES register the composition at our PRO (BMI/ASCAP) once we have writer IPIs
- DSR DOES confirm the publisher split (100% publisher share → DSR Publishing)

---

## MLC (Mechanical Licensing Collective)

MLC handles digital mechanical royalties from US streaming (Spotify, Apple, etc.).

- DSR Publishing (ASCAP) has an MLC account — Thomas needs the publisher number to confirm
- LAB10 (BMI) needs an MLC account created — pending
- Artists do NOT need their own MLC account — the publisher handles this
- For cover songs: MLC pays the ORIGINAL publisher only, not DSR
