## Parsed Offer Data from MyShowsapp.xlsx

### Column Key (MY SHOWS sheet)
- **Col A**: Checkbox 1 (likely "Paid" or "Completed")
- **Col B**: Checkbox 2 (likely "Confirmed" or "Contracted")
- **Col D**: Show description (date + city + notes)
- **Col E (AMOUNT)**: Total offer amount ($)
- **Col F**: When present instead of G, appears to be an alternate commission split
- **Col G (PRYSM)**: Management commission (10% of offer)
- **Col H (TOM)**: Agent/booking commission
- **Col I (TIM)**: Second agent commission (rarely used)
- **Col J (LEE)**: Artist payout (sometimes shows partial payments)
- **Col K (NOTES)**: Travel/logistics notes
- **Col L**: Contract/offer status + HGR (hospitality/green room rider)
- **Col M**: Additional notes (deposit info, announcements, etc.)
- **Col N**: Assets status
- **Col O**: Advance info
