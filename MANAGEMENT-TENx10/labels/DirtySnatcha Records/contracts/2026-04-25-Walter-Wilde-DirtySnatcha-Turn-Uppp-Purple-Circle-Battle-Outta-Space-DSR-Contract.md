## 5. ROYALTIES

**Net Receipts:** Label shall pay Artist fifty percent (50%) of Net Receipts received by Label from the exploitation of the Masters ("Artist's Royalty"). Net Receipts are defined as gross receipts actually received by Label from third parties, less Label's reasonable and documented costs including but not limited to distribution fees, marketing expenses, and production costs.

**Mechanical Royalties:** Artist acknowledges that mechanical royalties from US streaming are collected by the MLC and paid to Publisher (DSR Publishing) under a blanket license. Artist's share of mechanical income is encompassed in the Net Receipts split above.

**Accounting:** Label shall provide royalty accountings semi-annually via the distributor's split pay system (currently Label Engine / Ingrooves). Accountings shall be delivered within ninety (90) days following each June 30 and December 31.
