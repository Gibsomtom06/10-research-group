## What the management business does (rough)

- **Manages** the 4 roster artists above end-to-end: bookings, releases, royalty admin, content, contracts, financials.
- **Operates** DSR (one of the labels under it) — A&R, catalog, releases, distribution via VMG.
- **Uses** TENx10 the platform (`products/tenx10-platform/`) as the operational tooling. DSR is the live proof-of-concept.
- **Sells** the platform to other managers/labels who want the same operational capability.

The business and the platform are mutually reinforcing: real management work generates real product feedback; the platform is the tool the business runs on.
