# Example File Analysis Notes

## 1. Crankdat x Tabernacle Marketing Letter (PDF)

This is a **comprehensive marketing expectations document** sent to a promoter. It is the gold standard for what our Show Onboarding Agent should produce.

### Structure:
- **Header:** Event name, headliner, support, venue, date
- **Timeline:** Announce & sign-up date, artist presale (with password), promoter presale, general on-sale — all with specific dates and times
- **Ticket Text:** Exact formatting for how the ticket should read (promoter small font, artist large font, tour name large font)
- **Recommended Budget Allocation:** Announce/On-Sale 40%, Maintenance 10%, Backend 50% — matches our 4-phase system exactly
- **Ad Planning:** Links to a Google Sheets ad planning doc with channels, timelines, targeting, geotargeting, artist affinity lists
- **Ad Reporting Doc:** Links to a live Google Sheets reporting doc with deadlines
- **Social Media Requests:** Specific dates for social support (follow-up CTA, new music release, 1 month away maintenance, weekly support, day of show)
- **Content Ideas:** Carousels, artist highlight, support highlight, IG takeovers, countdowns, custom graphics (needs approval), giveaways
- **CRM Sends:** Specific email/SMS sends the promoter should execute at each phase (presale, on-sale, 1 month away, week-of) with audience targeting (past purchasers, affinity, excluding current buyers)
- **Additional Callouts:** Checklist of questions for the promoter — digital budget confirmation, ad checkpoints, street team, bundle posts, custom graphics capability, sponsors/media, university engagement, dark ads recommendation
- **Approved Assets:** Links to press photos, logo, approved content/footage, approved performance photos, approved music — all hosted on Dropbox/Google Drive

### Key Takeaway:
This is extremely professional and thorough. Our system needs to produce something at this level or better, customized per show.
