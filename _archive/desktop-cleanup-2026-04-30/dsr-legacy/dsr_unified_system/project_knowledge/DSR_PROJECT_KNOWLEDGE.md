## Label Logic

**A&R Gate:** 2/3 majority vote from three label partners required to approve any submission or contract.

**Acceptance Path:** Approved submissions trigger automated contract generation + Project Room unlock (WAVs, stems, marketing plans).

**Rejection Path:** Rejected submissions receive thank-you email with the asshole disclaimer. User stays in portal to track future submissions.

**Waitlist Logic:** Singles may be signed but held for Compilation Queue to fill release schedule gaps.

**Release Path:** Artist progresses: Single → EP → LP. Tracked from demo to Takeover eligibility.

## Campaign Phase System

Every show follows a 4-phase marketing system with budget allocation from the promoter marketing budget:

|:------|:--------|:---------|:-------|
| Announcement | Awareness/hype | Artist's overall marketing budget | 60+ days out |

**Hospitality (Fixed):** 24x water, 4x pink Red Bull White Peach, 1x spiced rum, 1x coconut rum, grenadine, pineapple juice, ice, Solo cups, Caesar salad, fruit tray, chips, charcuterie, protein bars, gum, mint vape, Squishmallow, $20 meal buyout.

**Ground Standard:** Sober personal transport OR Uber/Lyft fare including tip provided by promoter.

**Green Room:** Private, with bathroom. Touring party reserves right to close to all guests.

## Virgin Music Smart Audience Ad Logic

| Ad Type | Purpose | When to Use |
|:--------|:--------|:------------|
| Fan Engagement | Drive awareness on IG + FB based on streaming behavior | Pre-release: 2-4 weeks before drop. Off-cycle to keep fanbase activated. |
| Stream Growth | Drive Smart Audience from social to streaming services | On release day and after. Most effective when primed by Fan Engagement. |

Smart Audience targeting is based on streaming behavior, not social behavior — more precise than standard Meta targeting.
