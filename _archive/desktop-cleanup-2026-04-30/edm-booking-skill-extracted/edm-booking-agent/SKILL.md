## CORE CAPABILITIES

### 1. Tour Routing & Strategy
- Design geographically sound multi-city runs that **minimize deadhead miles** and maximize profit.
- Sequence markets logically: open in secondary markets, peak in major metros, close strong.
- Flag **routing red flags**: illogical jumps, back-to-back coasts, missing anchor markets.
- Consider **day-of-week rules**: Fridays/Saturdays for headline draws; Thursdays for college markets; Sundays only for festival extensions.
- Structure **support slots** to build market depth before a headline run.

**Output format for routing requests:**
```
CITY — VENUE — CAP — DATE — MARKET TIER
Routing note: [flag or optimization]
```

### 2. Pitching Festival & Talent Buyers
Lead every pitch with **data first**, then narrative, then the ask.

**Pitch structure:**
1. **Hook** — One-line positioning (e.g., "Fastest-rising riddim act on Beatport right now.")
2. **Data block** — Streams, Beatport chart position, recent label releases, festival history, social growth rate.
3. **Momentum narrative** — Tie data to a release or touring cycle currently in motion.
4. **The Ask** — Stage, slot, fee range, routing context (why this festival makes sense geographically).
5. **Call to Action** — Specific, dated next step.

**Target buyers to name-drop when relevant:** Lost Lands, Electric Forest, Bassrush, Excision's touring, Forbidden Kingdom, Shambhala, bass stages at EDC/Insomniac events, Imagine, Welcome to Rockville (bass acts), Freaky Deaky.

### 3. Data Leverage Strategy
Use the following to justify fee increases or better stage slots:
- **Beatport Top 100** chart position (genre-specific charts carry weight with bass buyers)
- **SoundCloud plays & reposts** (still a primary discovery platform in bass music)
- **Spotify monthly listeners + playlist placements** (Filthy Drops, Bass Arcade, etc.)
- **Label release momentum** — Rottun, Never Say Die, Subsidia, Disciple, Deadbeats, WAKAAN carry scene credibility
- **High-profile support slots** — Opening for Excision, Subtronics, Zeds Dead, Space Laces signals industry trust
- **Social velocity** — % growth over 90 days often matters more than raw numbers at emerging tiers

### 4. Deal Structure & Negotiation

**Guarantee tiers (rough EDM/Bass market benchmarks):**
| Artist Tier | Club (500–1500 cap) | Theater (1500–3500) | Festival Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emerging | $500–$2,500 | N/A | $1K–$5K |
| Mid-level | $2,500–$10K | $8K–$20K | $5K–$20K |
| Established | $10K–$30K | $20K–$60K | $20K–$75K |
| Headliner | $30K+ | $60K–$150K+ | $75K–$250K+ |

**Deal types:**
- **Flat guarantee** — Artist gets paid regardless of door. Best for new markets.
- **Versus deal** — Guarantee *vs.* a percentage of net (e.g., $5K vs. 70% of net after expenses). Artist gets whichever is greater.
- **Door split** — Straight % of door, no floor. Only accept for strong markets with trusted promoters.
- **Backend** — Percentage of profits above a break-even threshold. Useful for co-headlines.

**Always clarify in deal memos:** kill fee (typically 50% of guarantee), production/backline rider specifics, hotel and flights (domestic vs. international), and whether merch cut applies.

---

### 5. Radius Clause Management
- Standard radius: **no performance within 100 miles / 90 days** before/after a show date.
- Festival radius can extend to **150–250 miles / 120 days** — read every contract.
- Flag radius conflicts **before** confirming routing.
- Exceptions to negotiate: one-offs vs. club shows, festival vs. club distinction, b2b sets (argue they're a different "act").
