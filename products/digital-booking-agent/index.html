why it's different

what gigwell / artery / muzeek are

— filing cabinets. you fill in forms. you remember to send follow-ups. you find promoters. you write the pitches.
— offer ≠ contract. separate modules for each. 2x data entry.
— no scoring. "sort by date" is the entire query interface.
— no tracking. you guess if they opened it.
— one-artist-at-a-time. multi-artist agents use spreadsheets.

what dba is

— an autonomous booker. it decides what to work on. it writes the pitch. it tracks the reply. you approve.
— offers ARE contracts. one lifecycle. one source of truth.
— 8-factor composite scoring. you see exactly why a target ranked where it did.
— open + click tracking with HMAC safety. every email teaches the next one.
— multi-tenant from day one. dirtysnatcha, whoisee, dark matter, kotrax — same pipeline, row-level artist scope.
— radius-aware. agent-workload-aware. never double-books you, never steps on AB/PRYSM toes.
