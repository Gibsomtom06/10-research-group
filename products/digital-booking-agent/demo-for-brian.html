The workflow DBA runs for each artist

Six steps. You review #6.

This is what happens for a single artist on a single morning. Multiply by 30 artists. Everything happens before you open your laptop.

1
Routing window scan
Pulls open dates from the artist's calendar, cross-refs tour anchors, identifies routing gaps where a show would fit cleanly. Outputs: 5-12 candidate markets.
automatic · morning
2
Promoter pool match
For each candidate market, pulls the warmest relevant promoter from the contact graph (insider → warm → cold tier, history with the artist, reply rate). Flags anyone two agents are already working.
automatic
3
6-step decision engine
Guarantee floor check, market tier viability, CPT projection, calendar conflict, promoter credibility, marketing commitment verification. Each step logs its reasoning so you can audit any decision in 10 seconds.
visible reasoning
4
Concept-aware pitch draft
Writes the email. Per-artist voice rules (DirtySnatcha = guarantee-first no door splits, Dark Matter = door splits OK market-dependent). No AI giveaways — no em-dashes, no filler phrases, no "I hope this finds you well". Tier-aware tone (insider = clipped, cold = structured).
Claude-powered
5
Inbound offer parse
When a promoter replies with an offer sheet, DBA extracts: guarantee, deposit %, radius, HGR (hotel/ground/rider), capacity, show date. Surfaces the relevant counter-points. Stamps the status on the kanban.
automatic
6
Your approval
You open /drafts in the morning. See the pitches ready to send, each with confidence score + decision trace. Approve / edit / reject. Approved drafts fire Tue/Wed/Thu 9-11am in the recipient's local timezone (weekday-morning gate for deliverability).

What the first 30 days look like

No big-bang launch. We prove it on 3 artists first.

Day 0
Handshake + pilot scope.Pick 3 of your 30 potential automated artists — ideally ones where you know you're dropping balls. Sign a 2-page performance-share agreement.
Day 1-3
Gmail + calendar connect.OAuth your sending inbox + each artist's Google calendar. DBA ingests the last 90 days of offer threads per artist. You review the imported contact list.
Day 4-7
First DBA pitches go out.I seed the first 15 pitches across the 3 artists. You review each in /drafts. Approve, edit, or reject. Approved ones ship on the Tue/Wed/Thu 9-11am gate.
Day 8-14
First replies + inbound offers.DBA parses inbound offers, stamps HGR, lifts onto kanban. You see the same 6-column offer lifecycle you already run mentally, but it's happening without you typing anything.
Day 15-21
Counter-offers auto-drafted.When a promoter replies with a weak offer, DBA drafts the counter based on your tune of the minimum acceptable terms. You approve before send.
Day 22-28
First deal memos generated.DBA turns a verbal confirm into a signable PDF deal memo with your (Corson) standard terms. You review + counter-sign. Deposit tracking starts.
Day 30
Review meeting.We pull the numbers. Pitches sent, replies, offers, confirms, dollars. If the pilot is working, we expand to the other 27 artists over the following 60 days. If not, we stop.

One 30-min call. We pick the 3 pilot artists.

Not a sales pitch. A logistics call — names, calendars, who owes you a follow-up, which markets you've been meaning to reopen. By the end I'll have enough to fire the first batch of DBA pitches within a week.
