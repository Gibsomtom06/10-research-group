## MHP Processes to Build

### Process 1 — Custom Merch Order + Mockup Generation
- Customer (artist, label, brand) submits: art file + product type + size/color
- System generates mockup (AI-assisted, Gemini Nano 2 candidate)
- Customer approves mockup
- Order placed with print vendor (Printful or equivalent)
- Prototype shipped

### Process 2 — Pre-Order + Fan Ordering Flow
- Artist sets up pre-order listing with mockup
- Fans place pre-orders
- System batches orders and submits to vendor at threshold
- Fulfillment tracked and communicated to buyers
