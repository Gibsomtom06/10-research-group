What we do

Full-service wheel repair, custom coating, and OEM refurbishment.

Local in-shop work for Metro Detroit drivers. National shipping on our ready-to-install OEM inventory. One-stop — because the shop next door won't take your tire off the rim.

🛠️

Structural Wheel Repair

Bent rims, cracks, welds, straightening. Most "totaled" wheels are recoverable for a fraction of OEM replacement cost.

🎨

Custom Powder Coating

Chemical strip, sandblast, electrostatic powder spray, 400°F cure. Any color — OEM match or custom. Also: calipers, suspension, full frames.

⚡

Curb Rash & Cosmetic

Scratches, nicks, dings, road rash. Machined or painted finishes restored. Most cosmetic work turns in 2 days when you drop the vehicle.

🔧

Mount & Balance on Vehicle

Full service — we pull the tire, fix the rim, balance, and remount on your car. No loose-rim drop-offs required. Indoor vehicle storage while we work.

🚗

OEM Wheel Replacement

When a wheel is beyond repair, we stock refurbished factory OEM replacements at a fraction of dealer price. Ships nationwide.

🏠

Secure Indoor Storage

Leave your car with us while we work. Indoor, climate-controlled bay. No risk to paint, wheels, or interior while you're without the vehicle.
