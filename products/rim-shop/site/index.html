How it works

Drop it Monday. Pick it up Tuesday.

When you leave the vehicle with us, we handle everything — removal, repair, coating, balancing, and remount. Two days, start to finish.

01

Drop Off

Bring the vehicle (or loose wheels). We inspect and confirm the repair scope. You get an exact quote before we touch anything.

02

Removal

We safely remove tires and wheels. Your vehicle is stored indoors in our climate-controlled bay while we work.

03

Repair & Coat

Strip, blast, repair, prime, powder coat at 400°F, and apply our patented protective clear coat. OEM-level finish quality.

04

Balance & Mount

Tires are remounted, precision-balanced, and torqued to spec. Your vehicle is ready — not just the rims.
