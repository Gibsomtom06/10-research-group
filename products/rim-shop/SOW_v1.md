# Statement of Work — Performance-Share Pilot

**Between:** 10 Research Group ("10RG"), and Wheel Repair Specialists of Michigan ("WRS")
**Effective date:** _____________, 2026
**Pilot term:** 90 days from effective date
**Document:** 1 of 1

---

## 1. What 10RG will do

Over the 90-day pilot, 10RG will design, build, launch, and manage the following for WRS at no upfront cost to WRS:

1. **Google Merchant Center feed + Shopping/Performance Max campaigns** for WRS's refurbished OEM wheel inventory.
2. **Local Google Search Ads** targeting a 25-mile radius of 10500 W 8 Mile Rd for high-intent service keywords (powder coating, wheel repair, curb rash, mount & balance).
3. **Refinishing prioritization agent** — weekly work order ranking WRS's "Not Ready" inventory by search demand × price, so WRS knows which units to refinish first.
4. **AI chatbot** on WRS's website for inventory matching, photo-based repair quotes, and abandoned-cart recovery triggers.
5. **Abandoned-cart email sequence** (3 emails over 72 hours).
6. **eBay store audit + optimization** — inventory aligned with the GMC feed, promoted listings managed.
7. **Weekly 30-minute review call** — numbers, what's working, what's next.

## 2. What WRS will do

1. Provide read access to: current inventory list, pricing, website admin, Google Ads / Merchant Center accounts (or authorize 10RG to create them under WRS ownership), and the existing eBay seller account.
2. Expedite the prioritized "Not Ready" units 10RG flags each week so ad spend has product to sell.
3. Honor the pricing list 10RG builds campaigns around (powder coating tiers, refurbished wheel prices).
4. Respond to chatbot-captured service leads within 24 hours during business days.
5. Ship e-commerce orders within WRS's standard turnaround.

## 3. How 10RG gets paid

**Zero upfront. Zero retainer. Zero hourly.**

For every sale generated through the channels 10RG builds and manages, WRS pays 10RG the **margin above WRS's wholesale cost**, net of ad spend.

- "Wholesale cost" = WRS's direct cost of goods + direct labor/energy for that unit. Documented per SKU category at start of pilot.
- "Sale generated through 10RG channels" = attributable to Google Ads, Merchant Center, the on-site chatbot, the abandoned-cart sequence, or eBay listings managed by 10RG during the pilot window.
- "Ad spend" is paid by WRS as a pass-through (invoiced monthly with receipts) and is deducted before margin is split to 10RG.

**Settlement cadence:** every two weeks. 10RG provides a reconciliation report showing attributed orders, WRS-stated wholesale cost per order, ad spend, and the margin balance due.

**Caps:** none. Upside is uncapped to both parties.

## 5. Ownership + handover

- All ad accounts, Merchant Center accounts, domain properties, and email-sending reputation are created under or linked to WRS ownership from day one.
- All content (ad creative, product images, chatbot flows, email copy) is WRS's property on creation.
- 10RG retains the right to reference WRS as a pilot client and to describe the engagement structure and aggregate results in case studies and marketing, **without** disclosing WRS's wholesale costs or margins unless WRS gives written permission.
- At pilot end, WRS keeps everything. If we don't extend, 10RG hands over all access and walks away.

## 6. Exit + extension

- **Either party can exit at any time with 14 days written notice.** Obligations for sales already attributed during the pilot window remain.
- At day 90, both parties review results and decide whether to:
  (a) Extend under the same performance-share terms,
  (b) Convert to a fixed monthly retainer, or
  (c) End the engagement.

## 7. What this SOW does NOT cover

- Physical goods, refinishing labor, or shipping — that remains WRS's domain.
- Customer service beyond the chatbot's first-touch capture — WRS handles fulfillment and post-sale.
- Any work on properties not listed in Section 1.
