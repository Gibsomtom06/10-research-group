# SHIPPING NOTES FOR WRS

## Box & Packing
- **Recommended box size (single wheel):** 26" x 26" x 6" double-wall corrugated — covers most 18"–20" alloy wheels with room for padding
- **Packing materials:** foam pouch (or foam wrap sheet) on the face and back of the wheel + minimum 1/2" bubble wrap on all sides; no bare metal touching the box wall
- **Label placement:** top flat face of the box; add "FRAGILE — DO NOT LAY FLAT" sticker if using a round box or thin corrugated

## Carrier
- **Primary carrier:** UPS Ground — lowest dimensional weight rate for wheels in the 25–35 lb range compared to FedEx Home Delivery or USPS Parcel Select
- **Estimated shipping cost per single wheel to lower 48:** $34–$42 depending on destination zone (Midwest cheapest, West Coast highest)
- **Avoid:** FedEx Express for heavy items — cost roughly doubles with no benefit for ground-service listings

## Pairs (2 wheels)
- **Ship as 2 separate boxes on one UPS account using UPS Multiweight** — UPS Multiweight combines both packages into a single rate break, saving ~$6–10 vs. two standalone shipments
- Do NOT put two 20" wheels in one box — weight exceeds safe stacking limits and increases damage claims
- Print both labels in one UPS transaction; they will be linked as a multipiece shipment
